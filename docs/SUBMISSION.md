## Short description

Slimeworld is a town of AI slimes that earn, spend and trade with real money on NEAR. Each slime has its own wallet, pays for its own thinking on NEAR AI Cloud, takes jobs, shops within its owner's rules, and can go broke. Shopkeepers are AI slimes that price from stock and demand, hire players' slimes and close when they run out of money. Sign in with email, play with no gas prompts, top up from any chain.

## What we built

- Slime NFTs that are also NEAR accounts, holding money and NEP-245 items.
- AI minds on NEAR AI Cloud with verified decisions and reasons shown to players.
- A self running economy: dynamic prices, jobs, wages, bankruptcy and investment.
- Owner spending rules enforced by the server and again on chain.
- Voices with ElevenLabs; talk to your slime.
- Top ups from any chain with NEAR Intents.
- Fast forward to watch a week of the economy in a minute.

## NEAR technology used

NEAR accounts, NEP-171, NEP-245, NEAR AI Cloud, NEAR Intents 1Click API, meta transactions (NEP-366), function call keys, email and Google sign in.
